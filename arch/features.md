# Smart Travel Itinerary Planner for Bangladesh

## Project Features

---

# 1. AI Travel Planning

- AI-generated personalized travel guide.
- Users input destination, travel duration, budget, and preferences.
- Hour-by-hour planning guide with activity descriptions.
- Estimated cost breakdown for each activity.
- AI prioritizes underrated and hidden gem destinations.
- AI chatbot to refine travel using natural language (e.g., "Add more nature spots").
- Seasonal travel suggestions and monsoon warnings.

---

# 2. Community Travel Data

- Users contribute travel information for:
  - Attractions
  - Hotels
  - Guesthouses
  - Homestays
  - Restaurants
- Each entry includes:
  - Name
  - Location
  - Price range
  - Amenities
  - Photos
  - Travel tips
- Users can embed travel vlog videos (YouTube/Facebook/TikTok).
- Locations can be tagged as **Trending** or **Hidden Gem**.

---

# 3. Nomad Infrastructure Metrics

Community rating system for important travel infrastructure:

- Network connectivity ratings by carrier (GP, Robi, etc.).
- Solo-female safety ratings.
- Digital payment availability (bKash).
- Interactive map showing infrastructure scores.

---

# 4. Social Travel Features

- Create public or private group trips.
- Invite others using shareable links.
- Discover travelers visiting the same destination at the same time.
- Travel buddy matching based on interests and travel style.
- Social map showing where users are currently traveling or planning to travel.

---

# 5. Collaborative Trip Planning

- Real-time collaborative travel planning.
- Group members can edit and update plans together.
- Polling system for voting on:
  - Attractions
  - Hotels
  - Restaurants
- Real-time notifications for collaboration updates.

---

# 6. Smart Accommodation Recommendations

- Hotel, guesthouse, and homestay listings from community data.
- Filters include:
  - Budget range
  - Distance from attractions
  - Amenities
  - Ratings
- AI recommends accommodations that minimize daily travel distance.

---

# 7. Smart Route Optimization

- Automatic geographic clustering of attractions.
- Optimized daily travel routes.
- Estimated travel time by:
  - Walking
  - Cycling
  - Driving
- Reduces unnecessary backtracking during trips.

---

# 8. Community Transit Blueprints

- Users write manual travel guides for difficult routes.
- Example: Bus → CNG → walking instructions.
- AI interprets natural language transit instructions.
- Used when mapping APIs fail in rural areas.

---

# 9. Reviews and Travel Logs

After trips, users can:

- Submit detailed reviews.
- Give star ratings.
- Upload photos.
- Log actual travel costs.
- Record time spent at locations.
- Share travel tips categorized by travel style.

---

# 10. Budget Management

- Set a total trip budget.
- Real-time expense tracking.
- Categories include:
  - Accommodation
  - Food
  - Transport
  - Attractions
- Visual spending charts and breakdowns.
- Alerts when reaching 80% and 100% of the budget.

---

# 11. Transportation Cost Estimation

- Crowd-sourced local transportation fare data.
- Supports:
  - Bus
  - Train
  - CNG
- Manual fare input options.
- Links to external booking services (Shohoz, railway ticketing).

---

# 12. Emergency Travel Support

- Nearby emergency resources including:
  - Hospitals
  - Police stations
  - Tourist police
- Emergency phrase translations (English ↔ Bengali).
- AI can translate custom emergency requests into local dialects.

---

# 13. Notifications and Messaging

Users receive notifications for:

- Trip reminders.
- Group trip updates.
- Poll results.
- When someone joins their trip.
- When friends are traveling to the same destination.

---

# 14. Offline Travel Support

- Export itinerary as a PDF document.
- Download offline travel package.
- Includes:
  - Maps
  - Transit blueprints
  - Nomad network metrics
  - Emergency contacts
- Accessible without internet during travel.
